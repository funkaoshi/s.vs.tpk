[Wyrdcry][1] has a competitor, and it’s a tough one: [Black Death City by Sean Parsons][2]. The fellow who made [Warcry][] is making his own skirmish game, his own take on [Mordheim][]. In what feels like a plot twist, it’s more complicated than I had expected, borrowing from Kill Team and some of the old school mechanics of Warhammer. I need to give the game a try, because Parsons is probably one of the best game designers working right now. It’s an exciting time for Mordheim fans. [His most recent video is about releasing this game for public play testing.][3]
