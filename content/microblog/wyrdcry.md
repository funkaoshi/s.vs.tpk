[Wyrdcry 1.0][1] is out. The game is a mashup of [Warcry][] and [Mordheim][], two of the greatest war games of all time. The game is available for free. Game play is almost identical to Warcry, so that is to say basically perfect. Deeper list building options and the factions you can play are what pull this towards Mordheim.
